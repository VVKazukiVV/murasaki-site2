Our Project

Welcome to the official page of Murasaki Games organization! We are currently developing an exciting, high-performance cross-platform multiplayer game.

Game Overview

Our upcoming title is a dynamic 3D party game inspired by the mechanics of Ultimate Chicken Horse, built on Unreal Engine 5. Players build the levels in real-time, with sub-levels loading seamlessly directly into the main map.

Key Features

  • Cross-Platform Play: Full multiplayer support between PC and Mobile devices.
  • Maximum Performance: Highly optimized LowPoly AtlasGradient art style with Lumen disabled to ensure high framerates on all supported devices.
  • Epic Online Services: Powered by EOS for secure authentication, cross-play matchmaking, and reliable Listen Server connections.
  • Split-Screen Support: Play with your friends on a single screen or join online lobbies.
