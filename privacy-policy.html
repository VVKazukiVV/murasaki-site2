# PRIVACY POLICY **Last Updated: October 13, 2026** This Privacy Policy explains how **Murasaki** ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in relation to our game **Fool Knights** (the "Game"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of our players. The Game utilizes Epic Account Services provided by Epic Games, Inc. ("Epic") to enable multiplayer features and social interactions. ### 1. INFORMATION WE PROCESS (EPIC ACCOUNT SERVICES) To provide the core gameplay, multiplayer capabilities, and social features, the Game requests access to the following data from your Epic Games account: * **Epic Account ID** (a unique identifier assigned by Epic) * **Display Name** (your in-game nickname) * **Avatar** (your profile picture) * **Friends List** (to allow you to find and play with your friends) ### 2. HOW WE USE AND PROCESS YOUR DATA We process this information solely for the following purposes: * Authenticating you within the Game. * Displaying your profile (name and avatar) inside the Game interface. * Facilitating online multiplayer sessions and enabling social interactions with your Epic Games friends. ### 3. DATA RETENTION AND STORAGE We do not store or maintain your personal data on any external or self-hosted servers. All data retrieved from Epic Account Services is processed strictly **"on-the-fly" in the temporary volatile memory (RAM)** of your device during your active gaming session. As soon as you close the Game, this data is completely cleared from the application's memory. ### 4. DATA SHARING AND THIRD PARTIES We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. Your data is used exclusively within the Game environment to support local gameplay and matchmaking features. ### 5. YOUR RIGHTS AND DATA DELETION (ACCOUNT UNLINKING) Under global privacy laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have the right to request the deletion of your personal data or revoke access to it. Since we do not store your data on our servers, there is no data history for us to manually delete. However, you can completely revoke our access to your Epic Games account data and remove the association with the Game at any time by following these steps: 1. Log into your **Epic Games Account Portal**. 2. Navigate to the **Apps and Accounts** section. 3. Find **Fool Knights** and click **Revoke Access** or **Remove**. If you have any questions regarding your privacy, need assistance with unlinking your account, or wish to verify your data status, you can contact us directly at: **aken_18@outlook.com**. We will respond to your request within the timeframe required by applicable law. ### 6. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our Game features or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted with a revised "Last Updated" date.
