PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Murasaki Games ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in Fool Knights when using Epic Online Services (EOS) and Epic Account Services.

1. INFORMATION WE PROCESS

Before using certain online features of Fool Knights, players may be redirected to an official Epic Games authentication page in their web browser to sign in to their Epic Games account and grant the permissions requested by the game.

After authentication, the game may access information made available through EOS, depending on the permissions granted and features used. This information may include:

  • Epic Account ID, used to identify players for online features.
  • Display Name and Avatar, used to display player profiles in the game.
  • Friends List and Online Presence, used to display friends and their online status.
  • Lobby and Session Information, used to find and join multiplayer lobbies.

We do not ask players to provide their Epic Games account passwords directly to us.

2. HOW WE USE DATA

We use information accessed through EOS to provide the online features of Fool Knights, including authentication, displaying player names and avatars, displaying friends and their online status, and finding and joining multiplayer lobbies.

We do not intentionally use this information for advertising or sell player information.

3. DATA RETENTION

We use information as needed to provide the game's online features. We do not intentionally save the player information described above to local files, maintain a separate player-account database, or operate a cloud-save service.

Information held only in the game's active memory is no longer available in that memory after the relevant session ends or the game closes. However, the game, EOS SDK, Unreal Engine, operating system, or associated software may create technical logs or cache files.

We retain information under our control only for as long as necessary for the permitted functionality of the game and applicable requirements. Players may request deletion of information held by us by contacting the address in Section 5.

4. DATA SHARING

Fool Knights uses EOS to provide authentication, social features, and multiplayer functionality. Information is processed through the relevant services to provide these features.

Depending on the feature and permissions granted, player names, avatars, online status, and lobby information may be visible to other players through the game's interface.

We do not intentionally sell or independently distribute player information to third parties.

For information about Epic Games' handling of personal information, please refer to the Epic Games Privacy Policy.

5. YOUR RIGHTS & DELETION

Players may revoke permissions granted to Fool Knights through their Epic account settings, where the relevant controls are available.
